## Diagnosis of the current unphysical result
The config burns Jet-A as ONE lean charge (phi=0.64) then adds tiny dilution (mix_fraction 0.04+0.05)
and weak wall loss. Adiabatic flame T at phi=0.64 / 1.85 bar / 700 K preheat = 2077 K [DERIVED]; wall
UA cannot remove the enthalpy excess, so T stays near-adiabatic at the probe and thermal NO builds to
~700-2500 ppm in 3-10 ms (equilibrium ~4700-5300 ppm) [DERIVED] — ~2 orders above real exhaust.
ROOT CAUSE: the model treats one lean premixed charge as the whole combustor. A real GT burns
near-stoichiometric in the primary then dilutes with the bulk of the air to a low overall phi.

## 2. Grounding the cooling
- Primary lever = dilution air: m_air/m_products ~2.5-3 -> mixed T ~1220-1285 K [DERIVED]. Keep primary
  residence <=3-4 ms before first dilution to cap thermal NO (~740-970 ppm at 3 ms vs ~2500 at 10 ms).
- Residual lever = wall loss via Dittus-Boelter (Nu=0.023 Re^0.8 Pr^0.3; Incropera & DeWitt) or
  Gnielinski for transitional flow. Worked UA for heated_ss_line (ID~4.57 mm, L=2.1 m): 5.5 W/K @0.5 g/s,
  9.5 @1 g/s, 22.9 @3 g/s [DERIVED]. The config's heated_ss_line UA=45*0.12=5.4 W/K MATCHES DB at
  ~0.5 g/s — the sample line cooling is already physical; the defect is the combustor having no dilution.

## 3. Expected NO/NO2 + the headline sample-line science
- Probe sanity: NO ~80-200 ppm wet [DERIVED]; NO2 ~1-10 ppm at the hot probe [DERIVED/SOURCED] (most
  NOx leaves as NO; Lefebvre & Ballal; Combustion Sci.&Tech. 9 (1974) NO2 formation in GT engines).
- CRITICAL CAVEAT: homogeneous gas-phase 2NO+O2->2NO2 in the cooled line is only ~1-2% at ppm levels /
  short residence [DERIVED]. The REAL measured sample-line NO2/NOx bias is dominated by
  wall-catalyzed + NO+HO2->NO2+OH (radical/wet) chemistry + PTFE NO2 absorption — per SAE ARP1256D a
  metallic probe converts NO2->NO and PTFE absorbs NO2. FAA's line (SS probe -> SS heated line -> PTFE
  to FTIR) has both artifacts. The skeletal gas-phase mechanism CANNOT quantitatively reproduce the
  line bias without a wall/surface sub-model. State this plainly in any results.

## 4. Twin-data (OSSE) recipe to replace the impossible station CSV
Framing: synthetic-twin / Observing-System Simulation Experiment — truth generated by the forward
model, noise added, inference recovers KNOWN parameters. Demonstrates estimator consistency /
identifiability / calibration; does NOT validate chemistry or the NO2 line-bias magnitude.
- Operating points: baseline (primary phi=1.0, overall phi=0.25, 700 K, 1.85 bar, dilution per S1) +
  3-5 overall-phi points spanning 0.20-0.30 for identifiability.
- Noise (Gaussian on wet mole fraction, %-of-reading + floor): CO2 +-2%, CO +-3% (floor ~2 ppm),
  NO +-3% (floor ~2 ppm), NO2 +-5% (floor ~1 ppm), H2O +-5-8%, O2 +-2% (if measured). Sources: FTIR
  verification study + EPA Method 320 (+-5% calibration tol). NO2 widened (near floor + sampling artifacts).
- Count: ~30-50 noisy replicates/point, 3-5 points -> ~100-250 rows. Run posterior-coverage check.
